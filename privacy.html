WonderNest privacy notice

WonderNest by ThinkNest Education · Last updated November 2026

WonderNest helps schools plan inquiry learning and lets children keep their wonders, thinking and work in one place. This notice explains what information WonderNest keeps, why, and who can see it. Each school that uses WonderNest decides how it is used with its children and is responsible for telling families.

What we keep

  • Teachers: email address, password (stored securely by our login provider), school, classes and the units they plan.
  • Children: first name only, class and a picture password. We do not collect surnames, dates of birth, addresses or email addresses for children.
  • Children's work: wonders, thinking routines, stories, reflections, self-assessments, and photos or voice recordings they choose to add.
  • Families and exhibition visitors: the name and message they choose to leave (for example "Mum" or "Grandpa").

Who can see it

  • Children see their own work, and classmates' work only when it is shared to the class garden, bookshelf or action wall (stories and actions are approved by the teacher first).
  • Teachers see the classes they teach. Teachers at another school cannot see anything.
  • School admins see an overview of teachers and classes, not children's work.
  • Families see only their own child's garden, through a private link the teacher gives them. Teachers can make a new link at any time.
  • Photos and recordings are stored with long, random web addresses that cannot be guessed or listed.

The AI helpers

  • Some features use an AI service (Anthropic's Claude) to suggest ideas to teachers and to give children short, kind thinking prompts.
  • Children's names are never sent to the AI. Only the text needed for the task (for example a child's question) is sent.
  • Teachers can turn the children's AI helpers off for any class.
  • AI suggestions are a starting point. Teachers check content before sharing it with children.

Where it is stored

Data is stored by Supabase (database, login and file storage) and the website is hosted on GitHub Pages with Cloudflare. These providers process data on our behalf.

Keeping and deleting information

  • Teachers can delete children, classes, entries and units at any time. Deleting a child deletes their work.
  • Families can ask the school to see or delete their child's information.

Questions

Please contact your child's school first. Schools can contact ThinkNest Education through thinknest.education.

This notice describes how WonderNest works. Schools should check it against the data protection rules that apply to them (for example the UAE Personal Data Protection Law) and their own policies.
