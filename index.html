How it works

From a big theme to a nest full of wonders.

Three simple steps that keep children's questions at the heart of every unit of inquiry.

1
🌍

Choose a theme

Pick one of the six transdisciplinary themes and the grade, then describe your unit in a few words.

2
✨

Get inspired

WonderNest suggests grade-appropriate central ideas, lines of inquiry, specified concepts, stories, thinking routines and SDG links. You choose what fits.

3
🪺

Teach and reflect

Plan each week with the inquiry cycle and differentiated success criteria, and bring in true stories from the Wonder Library.
